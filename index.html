Treasury Strategy

Last Updated: 26.11.26

Blankavalo™ is a fully independent treasury company that rules out external borrowing and derivative leverage altogether, accumulating BTT (BitTorrent Token) over the long term through spot holdings only. Any BTT held beyond the 0.01% mark is settled and distributed to our stakeholders (really, just our friends) every year.

Buy Low

We take full advantage of BTT being one of the cheapest cryptocurrencies around. Rather than trying to time the market, we buy spot in regular installments whenever the market is quiet.

Staking Rewards

We never sell the rewards earned from staking our BTT; we reinvest them in full. It is a slow but sure compounding loop in which a growing stack keeps producing more stack.

Mining Swaps

We mine a variety of mineable coins, then swap the rewards into BTT and add them to the treasury. With no outside capital, we grow our holdings on electricity and computing power alone.

Ecosystem Activity

We seed files and share bandwidth in the BTT ecosystem, and every BTT reward we receive in return goes straight into the treasury. We contribute to the ecosystem and accumulate at the same time.
